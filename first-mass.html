Your first Mass

What happens at Mass?

No secret handshake, no test at the door and no need to know the words. Here is the basic shape before you go.

← Back to visiting
  1. ArriveCome a few minutes early and choose any free seat. Sitting near the back can make it easier to observe.
  2. ListenThere are readings from the Bible, a short talk called a homily and prayers for the Church and the world.
  3. Stand, sit and kneelFollow others if you wish. Remaining seated is fine if you are unsure or need to.
  4. CommunionCatholics who are properly prepared receive Holy Communion. Visitors are welcome to remain seated.
  5. Leave freelyStay for a conversation or slip away quietly. Both are acceptable. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

The shape of the service

Gathering

The congregation stands, sings and makes the Sign of the Cross. There is a brief acknowledgement of sin and a prayer for mercy.

Listening to Scripture

Several Bible readings are proclaimed, followed by the homily. On Sundays, the congregation normally says the Creed.

The Eucharist

Bread and wine are brought to the altar. Catholics believe that, through the prayer of the Church, they become the Body and Blood of Christ.

Being sent out

After Communion there is a final prayer and blessing. The word “Mass” is connected with being sent to live what has been received.

The one thing to remember:

You are welcome to attend without pretending to believe, joining every action or receiving Communion.
