There is no entrance exam

I used to be Catholic.

Whether you drifted away, were hurt, disagreed or made choices you regret, you do not have to earn permission to take one step back.

← Back to all questions

You can begin quietly

You can walk into a Catholic church, attend Mass or sit in silence without explaining your history. You are not required to solve every disagreement before you cross the threshold.

A return can begin with one honest prayer, one conversation or simply turning up.

What about guilt or embarrassment?

Christianity is built around mercy, not the pretence that people never fail. Catholics believe reconciliation with God is a gift. A priest can explain Confession without forcing you into it and help you work out what applies in your circumstances.

A prayer for today:

“God, I do not know exactly how to come back. Show me the next honest and peaceful step.”

What if the Church hurt me?

Hurt should not be minimised. You may need time, boundaries and a trustworthy person who listens before giving advice. Returning to God does not require pretending that wrongdoing was acceptable.

The first person you speak to does not have to be the last. It is reasonable to seek someone safe, patient and responsible.
