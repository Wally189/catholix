WELCOME

Thinking of coming on Sunday?

You can simply turn up.

  • You do not need special clothes.
  • Nobody will test your beliefs at the door.
  • You may sit quietly and join in only when comfortable.
  • If you are not Catholic, stay in your place during Communion—or go forward for a blessing where that is the local custom.
  • Feeling nervous the first time is completely normal.

Your first Mass

No secret handshake. Here’s the basic shape.

  1. ArriveCome a few minutes early and choose any free seat.
  2. ListenThere are readings from the Bible, a short talk and prayers.
  3. Stand, sit, kneelFollow others if you wish. Remaining seated is fine if needed.
  4. CommunionCatholics who are properly prepared receive. Visitors are welcome to remain seated.
  5. Leave freelyStay for a chat or slip away quietly. Both are acceptable.
