I’m curious about the Church.

You do not need to know the words, agree with everything or be ready to join. Curiosity is enough for a first step.

What is the Catholic Church for?

Catholics believe Jesus Christ is alive and continues his work through a visible community. The Church gathers to worship God, hear Scripture, receive the sacraments, serve people in need and learn how to live as followers of Jesus.

It is not a club for people who have their lives sorted. It is made of imperfect people who need truth, mercy and one another.

What do Catholics believe?

At the centre is not an institution or a rulebook, but Jesus: truly God and truly human, crucified and risen. Catholics believe God speaks through Scripture and the apostolic faith of the Church, and meets people especially through Baptism, the Eucharist and the other sacraments.

You are allowed to observe.

You can attend Mass, sit quietly, listen and leave without giving your details or promising anything.

Why does it sometimes feel complicated?

The Catholic Church has a long history and its own vocabulary. That can make the front door harder to find. You do not need to understand everything at once. Start with Jesus, the basic shape of Mass and one trustworthy person who welcomes questions.
